1) HAR ITEM KA PICHHLA KHAREED AUR WOHI NAFA
   - Bill POS bhejte waqt har item ka khareed aur us par lage Wholesale / Parchoon rate yaad ho jate hain.
   - Agli dafa wohi item lagayein — ya POS ka bill EDIT karein — to line par likha aayega:
     "pichhli dafa khareed 114.43 · nafa W 2% · R 12.5%" aur naye khareed par WOHI NAFA khud lag jayega.
   - Yaad na ho to pehle jaisa POS ke maujooda rates se nafa lagta rahega.

2) LINE MEIN "BILL KI RAQAM" KA KHANA
   - Jo raqam pehle sirf dikhti thi, ab likhi bhi ja sakti hai. Aap 1546.53 likhein aur ginti 12 —
     khareed khud 128.88 ban jayega. Ginti ya khareed badlein to raqam khud badal jayegi.
   - AI abhi bhi bill ka "Rate" wala column hi uthata hai (jaisa aap ne kaha). Tax/discount wala hisaab
     chahiye to us line par bill ki Amt. likh dein — khareed theek ho jayega.

3) AI KA CHUNA HUA NAAM BHI KHUD YAAD
   - Jab AI khud humari list se sahi item chun le, wo naam ab bina "✓ Yaad kar lo" dabaye yaad ho jata hai
     (isi liye "BROAST" agli dafa bhool gaya tha — ab nahi bhoolega).
   - Aur agar AI ne ghalat item chuna aur aap "✏️ Badlein" se sahi chunein, to wo naam purane (ghalat) item
     se HATA bhi diya jata hai — ghalat naam chipka nahi rehta.
