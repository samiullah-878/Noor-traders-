   - Milta-julta mila to card PEELA rehta hai aur "📌 Yaad wale naam se milta julta" likha aata hai —
     ek nazar dekh kar ✓ dabayein, to ye nayi likhawat bhi yaad ho jati hai (agli dafa bilkul barabar).

   - AI ko sirf 400 items jate hain. Ab jin items ke naam yaad hain wo HAMESHA list mein (supplier ke
     items ke baad sab se pehle).
