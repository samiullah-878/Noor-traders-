Blue Khata v1.22.0 — cumulative changed files for v1.20/v1.21

INSTALL:
1. Replace ZIP files in Hisaab/Sale repository. Keep existing config.js and vendor folder.
2. Publish the complete firestore.rules in note-traders-khata-7ccc1 > Firestore > Rules. Do NOT use nt-traders (Staff attendance).
3. Every device: Sync / Update, reload, check version 1.22.0.
4. Malik login > Settings > Mulazim password > Sirf Purchase Mulazim. Set and confirm a separate password (minimum 10 characters). This also prepares supplier names for Purchase access.
5. Employee login: Mulazim > Sirf Purchase > username admin > Purchase password.
Full App employees select Full App and use their Full App password. Existing Full App password remains valid until changed. Use different passwords for the two scopes. Password changes revoke the replaced scope's credential; other scope remains unchanged.

CHANGES:
- Due Accounts red button is now in top navigation; removed from Daily Sale footer.
- Purchase has its own screen and restricted login. Restricted employees see purchase entries, supplier names/phones and purchase PDFs; no Khata balances, Daily Sale, Cash, reminders or full backup in their interface.
- Purchase allows cash/credit purchases, photos and new supplier creation. A new supplier appears in the main Khata with zero opening balance; purchase creates its normal accounting entry.
- Malik Settings > Purchase supplier list update refreshes names after supplier edits/imports. Run this after restoring a backup or adding suppliers outside Purchase.
- Employees cannot create, edit or delete previous-date financial entries on any screen. Pakistan date is used. Current-day purchase edit/delete and existing current-day cash permissions remain available. Malik can handle previous dates.
- Full device backup now asks for Malik login so restricted employees cannot download the entire business ledger. Click Backup Download then complete Malik login to download the saved device copy. Ordinary owner backup remains in Settings.
- Restrictions are enforced by the supplied Firestore rules, not just hidden buttons. Publish those rules before sharing Purchase credentials.
- Browser caches on a device previously used by Malik are not erased. Give restricted employees their own browser profile/device rather than sharing an owner's browser storage.
- No production passwords, Firebase data or deployment were changed here.

Auth mocks passed for wrong-scope rejection, legacy Full App login and revoked-session revalidation. Accounting/import and cash-transaction regression tests passed.
