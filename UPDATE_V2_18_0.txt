1) FAALTU RESULT KHATAM
   - "Mayo" likhne par "mop 500" jaisi cheezein AB NAHI aatin.
   - Naya usool: agar 3 ya zyada PAKKE match (naam / lafz ka shuru / code) mil jayein to
     andaze wale (1 harf ki ghalti wale) match bilkul nahi dikhte.
   - Ghalti wali spelling tab bhi chalti hai jab pakka match na mile: "mayonaise" -> Mayonise national.

2) JIS GODAM SE MAAL NIKALNA HAI — WAHAN 0 / MINUS STOCK WALE ITEM DIKHTE HI NAHI
   - Jo cheez us godam mein hai hi nahi, wo list mein nahi aayegi.
   - Agar sirf 0 stock wale hi match hon to neeche ek patti:
     "🚫 2 item mile magar Godam 1 mein stock 0 — phir bhi dikhayein" — ek tap se dikh jayenge.
   - "Se (nikle)" godam badalte hi list us godam ke hisaab se nayi.

3) PREMIUM ROWS — BARE CHIPS
   - Har result par: 🏷 godam ka naam · 📦 stock (hara = theek, peela = kam, laal = 0) ·
     "1 Ctn = 24" (carton wale items par) · #barcode.
   - Stock carton mein bhi: "📦 72 Pcs · 3 Ctn".
   - Jo harf match hue wo peele nishan (highlight) mein.

4) BARCODE LIKHTE / SCAN KARTE HI ITEM KHUD LAG JATA HAI
   - 8 ya zyada hindson ka poora code (scanner gun ya haath se) -> item foran list mein,
     search khana saaf, aur cursor SEEDHA tadad (Ctn/Pcs) ke khane par, purani tadad select.
   - Adhoora code likhein to normal search chalti rahegi.

5) KHALI KHANE PAR "AKSAR BHEJE JANE WALE"
   - Search par tap karte hi, bina kuch likhe, aakhri 6 items jo aap is phone se transfer karte hain
     (jin ka us godam mein stock hai) — seedha tap karke lag jayein.
   - Ye ginti sirf is phone par rehti hai, kisi doosre ka kaam kharab nahi karti.
   - Ye items search mein bhi zara upar aate hain (match ki jagah nahi leti — sirf barabri toot ti hai).

6) EK KE BAAD EK TEZ
   - Item chunte hi keyboard khula rehta hai aur cursor wapas search par — agla naam foran likhein.
   - Keyboard wale: ⬆⬇ se chunein, Enter = lag gaya, Esc = list band.
   - Search 70ms ruk kar chalti hai — hazaron item par bhi jhatka nahi.
