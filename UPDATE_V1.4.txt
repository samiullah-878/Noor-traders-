V1.4 — Upload guide
1. Extract ZIP and upload all files WITH vendor folder to your repository.
2. Firebase Console > project nt-traders > Firestore Database > Rules: paste the complete firestore.rules supplied here and Publish. GitHub upload alone does not publish these rules.
3. Open app > Sync / Update > reload. Footer must show v1.4.0.
4. Owner Settings > Mulazim password: enter a shared staff password (at least 10 characters), confirm and Save. Username stays admin. This changes Blue Khata staff access only; old Staff app records/passwords are not edited.
5. Employees can create entries only for today's Pakistan date. Past/future records remain viewable. Owner can backdate. Offline employee entries must sync the same Pakistan day; older queued writes will be rejected by the date rule and owner must review/re-enter them.
6. Summary camera: take up to 3 pictures, save, then add more if needed. Pictures attach to the selected date, without affecting money totals.
7. Purchase: take/select up to 3 goods + 3 bill pictures. Each image is compressed. Preview the bill to ensure its writing is readable.
8. Daily PDF includes selected day's roznamcha and purchase photos on separate photo pages. Account PDF includes that account's purchase/entry photos; expense PDF includes cash purchase photos.
Validation: UI scenarios, date rejection, password/session ordering and PDF Blob generation passed. Camera/canvas rendering on a physical Android phone and deployed Firestore rules have not been tested in this workspace. No live files or passwords were changed.
