1) BILL KI PATTI — sirf wohi line
   - Card ke upar bill ki tasveer ka sirf wo hissa jahan ye line hai (peela nishan ke sath).
   - Patti par tap = poori tasveer, SEEDHA usi line par zoom.
   - Poori tasveer: do ungliyon se zoom, ek ungli se khainchein, do dafa tap = zoom. "‹ Wapas Jaanch" = usi card par.

2) SAB KHANE DO QATAAR MEIN
   - Upar: CTN · PCS · Khareed/CTN · Khareed/PCS
   - Neeche: Wholesale/CTN · Wholesale/PCS · Parchoon/CTN · Parchoon/PCS
   - Har khane ke neeche chhota "pehle" — pichhli dafa ki qeemat. Kisi ek ko badlein, baqi khud theek.

3) CARTON SIZE KHALI HO TO "1 CTN ="
   - Jaise bassan / badam — ek dafa likh dein (misal 20). CTN ke khane chalu, hisaab khud:
     170 carton × 20 = 3400, khareed 6830 ÷ 20 = 341.5. App hamesha ke liye yaad rakhti hai.
   - Note: POS mein us item ka carton size jab tak khali hai, POS ko pieces hi jayenge (CTN sirf app mein).

4) TEZ CHALNA
   - "✓ Theek — agla ›" hamesha neeche chipka hua.
   - Card par ungli DAAYEIN khainchein = ✓ agla · BAAYEIN = pichhla.
   - Khirki ghalti se band ho jaye to POS Purchase par "🧾 Jaanch kholo (N baqi)" — wahin se wapas.

5) BILL KE NEECHE WALE TOTAL SE MILAN
   - Bill par ginti ka total ho (jaise کارٹن 201 · پیس 309) to app apni lines ka jama milati hai.
     Farq ho to laal: "koi line chhooti ya ghalat parhi?"
