KAISE CHALTA HAI:
 - PC har item ki 6 mahine ki BIKRI (hafte-wise, 30/90/180 din) aur PURCHASE (kitni dafa, pichhli: kab, kitna, kis supplier se)
   POS se PARHTA hai (SQL mein kuch nahi likhta) aur app ko bhejta hai.
 - "🤖 Order banao": jin items ka stock 14 din se kam ka hai (roz ki bikri ke hisaab se) -> AI ko: stock, roz ki bikri,
   8 hafton ka rujhan, pichhli purchase -> AI ~3 hafte ki munasib tadad tay karta hai aur SUPPLIER-WISE jama karta hai.
   AI na chale to seedha hisaab (3 hafte ki zaroorat, carton mein poora).
 - Har line ek supplier: 🧾 naam, neeche 4-5 chips har qatar mein — "Cheeni 50kg · 25 Bori".
 - Chip par DOUBLE TAP = "✓ Maal aa gaya" (hari, kati hui) · wahan TAP = gayab.
   Ek tap (ruk kar) = tadad badlo (0 = hatao).
 - Order sab (malik + Full mulazim) ko nazar aata hai; aakhri order dikhta hai; naya jab chahein.
SACH: counter ki sale ab poori aati hai (PC se). Naya item jis ki bikri ki history nahi, us ka andaza nahi lagta.
